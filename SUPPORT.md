## Request a direction

After confirmation, open a GitHub issue with the asset, network, public transaction hash, and specific work you want expanded. The first issue claiming an unclaimed confirmed inbound transaction hash receives its operational request attribution. This is not cryptographic proof of wallet ownership, and an attributed hash cannot be reused.

A donation can direct what additional work is requested, but it does not purchase ownership, financial returns, a delivery deadline, or unsafe, illegal, private, proprietary, or out-of-scope work.
